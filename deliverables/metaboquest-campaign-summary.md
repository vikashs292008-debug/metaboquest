**Tagline:** Good health education. Beautifully communicated.

## 1. Executive Directive
* **Primary Objective:** Help people understand that metabolic health involves multiple interconnected aspects of health, rather than relying on one number or measurement alone.
* **Tone:** Friendly, curious, inclusive, calm, and science-informed.
* **Key Message:** Metabolic health involves multiple aspects of health; one measurement alone cannot describe the whole picture.
* **Call to Action:** "Learn more from trusted health resources."
* **Visual Direction:** Modern editorial wellness design with interconnected shapes, abstract cellular motifs, crisp typography, and accessible color contrast.

## 2. Evidence Register & Primary Sources
All factual health assertions are mapped to primary government and clinical health organizations:


### [SRC-001] What Is Diabetes? Causes, Types, and Metabolism Overview
* **Organization:** National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK), NIH
* **Exact URL:** [https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Metabolism is the biological process converting food into energy. Glucose is the primary circulating sugar used by cells for fuel, regulated by pancreatic insulin secretion and liver glycogen storage. Cellular metabolism is an integrated network rather than a single metric.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003, CLM-006


### [SRC-002] Prediabetes – Your Chance to Prevent Type 2 Diabetes
* **Organization:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
* **Exact URL:** [https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/basics/prediabetes.html](https://www.cdc.gov/diabetes/basics/prediabetes.html)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Metabolic health risks are cumulative and interconnected. A single elevated reading does not determine lifelong outcome. Structured lifestyle habits including regular physical activity, balanced dietary patterns, and restorative sleep substantially enhance insulin sensitivity without extreme restriction.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-001, CLM-004, CLM-005


### [SRC-003] Healthy Diet Factsheet (Key Principles for Cardiometabolic Health)
* **Organization:** World Health Organization (WHO)
* **Exact URL:** [https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Dietary patterns high in diverse vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, and unsaturated fats support metabolic balance. No isolated miracle ingredient can substitute for overall nutrition, physical activity, and adequate rest.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-002, CLM-005


### [SRC-004] Metabolic Syndrome – Definition, Risk Factors, and Clinical Criteria
* **Organization:** MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine (NIH)
* **Exact URL:** [https://medlineplus.gov/metabolicsyndrome.html](https://medlineplus.gov/metabolicsyndrome.html)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Metabolic syndrome is defined by a cluster of co-occurring cardiometabolic factors: elevated fasting glucose, high serum triglycerides, low HDL cholesterol, elevated blood pressure, and abdominal waist measurement. Assessing multiple biomarkers together provides a significantly more comprehensive picture than any single measurement in isolation.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-004, CLM-006


### [SRC-005] Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans (2nd Edition)
* **Organization:** U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS / CDC)
* **Exact URL:** [https://health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines](https://health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Physical activity induces acute contraction-mediated glucose uptake into skeletal muscle independently of insulin. Regular moderate movement maintains postprandial glucose regulation and vascular elasticity across all body types.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-005


### [SRC-006] Standards of Care in Diabetes — Comprehensive Medical Evaluation
* **Organization:** American Diabetes Association (ADA)
* **Exact URL:** [https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/47/Supplement_1](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/issue/47/Supplement_1)
* **Review Status:** `VERIFIED`
* **Verified Clinical Finding:** Biomarkers including continuous glucose metrics, fasting glucose, and HbA1c require individual clinical context. Personal health assessments should be conducted with a qualified healthcare professional who considers individual medical history.
* **Supported Claim IDs:** CLM-004, CLM-006
